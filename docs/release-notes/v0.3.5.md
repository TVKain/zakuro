## What's new

- **Themes:** System Settings → Display → Theme picks Light, Dark, or follows Windows (the default). The title bar follows too.
- **Clearer Graphics settings:** Resolution, Display filter, Upscale textures and Custom textures come first, then Motion (frame rate limit, frame interpolation), then Advanced (graphics API, 3D rendering). Every setting has a one-line explanation at the bottom of the window, and each choice in a picker has a note of its own.
- **Changes without restarting:** switching the graphics API happens on Home right away, and custom textures turn on or off in a running game. Settings that still need a restart say so only when you've actually changed them.

## Changes

- **Lower input lag and smoother motion:** frames are shown as soon as they're ready and lined up with your display's refreshes. At 60 FPS, the time from pressing a button to seeing it drops from about 11 ms to 7 ms, and uneven frames go from about 1 in 10 to under 1 in 100. Uncapped no longer slows the game down.
- **Sound in sync with the picture:** sound now trails the picture by about 44 ms, down from about 146 ms, which you could notice.
- **Better sound quality:** sound is converted to your speakers' rate far more cleanly (tones that used to pick up audible distortion now come through clean), and the game's voices play back with smoother interpolation.
- **Sharper, more accurate picture:** the display filters blend colours the way light does and use better scaling kernels, especially when the resolution is higher than your screen. The four filters and their names are the same.
- **Faster recompiling:** recompiling a game takes about 30% less time and much less disk space.
- **New icons** in the dock, settings sidebar and pickers.

## Download

- `zakuro-setup-v0.3.5.exe` (30 MB): run it to install. Updating over an earlier install keeps your games, saves and settings.
- `zakuro-v0.3.5-windows-x64.zip` (9 MB): `zakuro.exe` alone, for running without installing. Recompiling needs the installer's Zig, or a C compiler of your own.

Settings and saves stay in `%APPDATA%\zakuro`. Save states made with earlier versions don't load in this one.
